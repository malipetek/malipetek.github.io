## Software help, by the minute.

**Human-approved, agent-built.**

You can vibe code most of it. The last mile is where you need a developer: the
bug it keeps bringing back, the deploy, the payments, the thing that only breaks
for real customers. Send me that part.

A one-minute fix is **$1.67**. No quote, no minimum.

## How it works

1. **You ask.** Sign in with your email and write what you need — in the portal,
   by email, or in the chat on the site. No call to get started.
2. **I approve.** I read every request myself. Nothing starts until I say so,
   and I tell you if it is the wrong thing to build.
3. **An agent builds.** An AI agent does the typing in a sealed copy of your
   project. It cannot publish, deploy, or touch anything live.
4. **I check, then ship.** I check the result before it goes anywhere. How
   closely is your call: a quick check that it runs, or every changed line.

## What it costs

You pay for the time I actually spend, billed by the second, plus what the AI
run cost, with no markup. No packages, no minimum.

The first five pilot jobs — on my own and demo projects — billed between

## What keeps it safe

- Nothing starts until I approve the request.
- The agent works on a sealed copy of your project. It cannot publish, deploy,
  or touch anything live.
- Set a budget and I will not pass it without asking.
- If a machine stops mid-job, the job goes back in the queue instead of
  stalling.
- Every message, change and decision is in one thread you can read.

## Who this is for

Business owners with a list of small things that never get to the top: a form
that does not send, prices that are out of date, a page that looks wrong on a
phone, an integration that broke. Also people who built most of a site or app
with AI and stalled at the part that needs a developer.

## Who this is not for

A six-week rebuild, a team that needs ongoing capacity, or anything that
requires a fixed quote and a statement of work before work starts. Those go by
the clock, with me on them — not on autopilot. If it is the wrong thing to
build, I will say so.
